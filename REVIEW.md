### Items Examined and Confirmed NOT to be Defects

1. **Absence of internal `_id` in API responses**:
   Inspected whether stripping MongoDB's `_id` from `employees` and `attendance_logs` was a defect. Confirmed it is strictly required by the specification: `emp_code` is the business identifier, attendance records are addressed by `(emp_code, date)`, and `_id` must never appear in API responses.
2. **String format for calendar dates and shift times**:
   Inspected whether storing dates as `"YYYY-MM-DD"` and shift times as `"HH:MM"` in MongoDB was a defect. Confirmed intentional: `DATA_MODEL.md` explicitly notes calendar dates and shift times are plain strings to avoid unintended timezone conversions.
3. **No endpoints to create `ABSENT` or `LEAVE` records**:
   Inspected whether endpoints for punching in as `ABSENT` or `LEAVE` were missing. Confirmed intentional: the specification states `ABSENT` and `LEAVE` records are back-office seeded or introduced via the regularization `PATCH` endpoint; punch-in only accepts presence statuses.
4. **Distinction between a missing record and an `ABSENT` record**:
   A calendar day with no attendance document is not treated as an `ABSENT` record; it simply has no log. This distinction is preserved in analytics calculations.
